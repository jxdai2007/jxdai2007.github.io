When a method claims to remove a capability from a language model, did it actually remove it, or just hide it? Most of my work is about answering that question carefully: building the evaluations that tell the difference, and testing removal methods against attacks they weren't designed for.

Now

  • Testing whether runtime interventions (activation steering, concept erasure, neuron dampening) remove capabilities or merely suppress them — extending the comparison in PISCES to the intervention families it left out.
  • Working through the ARENA curriculum.
  • Finishing a paper on group bias in aligned LLMs, from the NSF Trustworthy AI REU at RIT (advised by Prof. Ashique KhudaBukhsh). Short version: the bias wasn't removable. Every method we tried either damaged the model or quietly failed, and the standard benchmarks couldn't see the difference.
